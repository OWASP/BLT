I think a weekly schedule and a limit of around five open PRs would be reasonable starting points. We can adjust these based on the number of updates generated and the maintainers' review workload.


We should keep automerge disabled during the initial pilot until we're confident that CI reliably tests every affected project. Once the testing gaps are addressed, we could consider automerging only low-risk updates under clearly defined conditions.


I'd also be happy to volunteer to help with the Renovate configuration, investigate the ForkMesh integration, and review the first few batches of dependency updates. I think starting small and validating the workflow together would help us make this reliable before expanding automation.
