Hey everyone! We are opening up opportunities for everyone to contribute to our official social media channels. If you enjoy writing, sharing tech insights, or highlighting community wins, we want you on the BLT Newsroom team! 🗞️✨

Here is how the process works:

  • Topics & Deadlines: We will regularly share specific writing topics based on our current requirements, along with a submission deadline.
  • Submit Your Draft: Write your post based on the given topic and share your draft right here in the group before the deadline hits.
  • Review & Feedback: The lead project maintainer and social media maintainers will review all the submitted drafts.
  • Final Selection: The draft that gets the top reviews will be selected and published on the official OWASP BLT social media pages!

This is a fantastic way to build your portfolio, practice your technical writing, and get your work seen by a much wider audience. Keep an eye out for our first topic announcement soon! 🎨💻
