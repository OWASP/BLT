# Terms and conditions

This page documents general terms-related information for BLT users. It is not legal advice and does not replace the terms, notices, or policies shown by the active BLT deployment.

Users should follow the terms shown by the active BLT deployment and applicable OWASP policies. For legal or policy questions, refer to OWASP-provided policy resources or project maintainers.

## Account responsibilities

- Use accurate account information where the platform requires it.
- Keep your credentials private and do not share accounts.
- You are responsible for activity performed through your account.
- Do not create accounts or submit content through unauthorized automated methods unless the active deployment explicitly permits that use.

## Acceptable use

- Use BLT for lawful bug reporting, issue coordination, and community participation.
- Do not submit spam, abusive content, malware, destructive code, or unrelated material.
- Do not use BLT to harass users, maintainers, organizations, or community members.
- Do not use BLT in a way that violates applicable laws, OWASP policies, or the rules of the active deployment.

## Submitted content

Bug reports, comments, attachments, and profile details may be visible to other users depending on the workflow and deployment settings. Do not submit secrets, credentials, private data, or information you are not authorized to share.

When you submit content, make sure it is accurate, relevant to the issue, and respectful of other users and organizations.

## Optional platform features

Some BLT deployments may include features such as APIs, organization workflows, invitations, bug hunts, bidding, or other account-specific actions. Use those features only where enabled and only within the permissions granted to your account.

Do not assume payment terms, reward terms, service levels, data retention, or access rights unless they are explicitly stated by the active deployment or an applicable OWASP policy.

## Changes and current terms

The active BLT deployment may update its terms, notices, or workflows over time. If there is a conflict between this documentation and terms shown by the active deployment, follow the active deployment terms and OWASP-provided policy resources.
