# Invite users

Use invite workflows to bring users into BLT activities, organizations, or project spaces where invitation features are enabled.

## When to invite users

- A user needs access to an organization or team workflow.
- A maintainer wants to add someone to a coordinated activity.
- A bug hunt or project process requires additional participants.
- The active deployment provides an invite feature for your role.

## Main actions

1. Confirm that you have permission to invite users.
2. Collect the required invite information, such as name, username, email address, organization, or role, depending on the form.
3. Open the invite workflow where enabled.
4. Enter the required details and review them for accuracy.
5. Submit the invite.
6. Tell the invited user what they may need to do next, such as creating an account, signing in, or accepting an invitation.

## What to check

- Invite only users who should have access to the relevant workflow.
- Choose the least access needed for the user's role where role selection is available.
- Confirm email addresses or usernames before sending invites.
- Do not use invite features for unsolicited outreach.
