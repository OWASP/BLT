# Trademark search

The trademark search workflow helps users look up trademark-related information from the data source connected to the active BLT deployment. Search results are informational and are not legal advice.

## When to use this page

- You want to search for a trademark term or name.
- You need a starting point for reviewing possible trademark records.
- You want to refine a query before reviewing detailed results.
- You understand that legal or policy decisions require appropriate professional or organizational review.

## Main actions

1. Open the trademark search page.
2. Enter the search term or phrase.
3. Submit the search.
4. Review the results page.
5. Refine the search if the results are too broad or do not match what you need.

## What to check

- Confirm spelling and spacing before searching.
- Search results may depend on the connected data source and its freshness.
- Treat results as informational only.
- For legal questions, consult qualified legal counsel or the appropriate OWASP policy resources.
