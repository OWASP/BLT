# Sign up

Use the sign-up page to create a BLT account. An account may be required for submitting reports, commenting, tracking profile activity, or using organization-related workflows.

## When to use this page

- You are new to BLT and need an account.
- You want to report bugs under your own profile.
- You need access to actions that are only available to signed-in users.
- You were invited to join BLT or an organization workflow.

## Main actions

1. Open the sign-up page from the site navigation or login page.
2. Enter the required account and profile details shown by the form.
3. Review any terms, consent, or policy information displayed by the active deployment.
4. Submit the form.
5. If the platform asks for verification, complete the verification step before signing in.
6. Go to the login page and sign in with the account you created.

## What to check

- Use accurate account information so maintainers can contact you if needed.
- Keep your credentials private and do not share an account.
- Confirm that required fields are complete before submitting.
- If the form rejects a value, follow the validation message shown on the page.
