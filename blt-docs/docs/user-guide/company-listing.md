# Company listing

The company listing helps users browse organizations or companies represented in BLT. It is a starting point for finding organization pages and related report activity.

## When to use this page

- You want to find a specific organization.
- You want to browse companies with visible BLT activity.
- You need to open an organization dashboard.
- You are checking whether a company already has a page before reporting or triaging issues.

## Main actions

1. Open the company listing page.
2. Browse the visible organization entries.
3. Use search, filters, or pagination if the deployment provides them.
4. Select the relevant organization to open its dashboard or detail page.
5. Review the organization page before taking report or triage actions.

## What to check

- Confirm that the organization name, domain, or other identifier matches what you need.
- Search and filter results may depend on the data available in the active deployment.
- If you cannot find the organization, use the available reporting or maintainer workflow rather than creating duplicate or incorrect records.
