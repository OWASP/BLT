SIZZLE
Workflow

A simple rhythm for async teams.

Three steps keep teams aligned without turning daily progress into another meeting.

01

Join an organization

Members request access. Owners and admins approve the people who should be part of each organization.

02

Check in daily

Share completed work, today’s plan, blockers, and mood for the organization you’re working with.

03

Track momentum

Admins review participation, blockers, streaks, and missing check-ins from a focused dashboard.

Organizations

Every check-in belongs to the right team.

Users can belong to multiple organizations and choose where each check-in goes. That keeps OWASP BLT, GSoC groups, and contributor teams separate without forcing separate accounts.

Multiple organizations

One user can contribute to several orgs and switch context at check-in time.

Approval workflow

Pending requests stay separate until an admin approves membership.

Multiple admins

Owners can share admin work across mentors, maintainers, or team leads.

Org-specific history

Progress, blockers, and streaks are scoped to the organization.

Dashboard

Know who’s active, blocked, or missing.

The admin dashboard turns daily updates into an operational view: today’s check-ins, missing members, blocker trends, mood signals, and pending join requests.
