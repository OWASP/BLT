    How it works

    No sign-up and no middleman: the money goes from sponsor to developer through GitHub Sponsors.

    💻

    Developers: join the 1000

    1. Turn on GitHub Sponsors.
    2. Set a goal of $1,000 a month, so your progress shows here.
    3. Add your GitHub username to tomato1000.txt in a pull request.
    4. You get a seat at the next weekly refresh. Any developer, any project.
    ❤

    People: sponsor one

    Pick someone whose tool you use, or let the spotlight pick for you. $5 a month from 200 people is a seat filled. Tell them why you chose them.

    🏢

    Companies: back your stack

    If you ship on open source, sponsor the people who maintain it. GitHub Sponsors bills organizations by invoice. One $1,000 sponsorship fills a seat on its own.

    GitHub keeps sponsorship amounts private. Progress here comes from what each developer publishes: a monthly-dollar goal on their Sponsors profile, and their public sponsor count otherwise. Sponsoring someone pays that person directly; it is not a donation to the OWASP Foundation or to an OWASP project (see the funding page for those). Tomato 1000 is an OWASP BLT community effort, open to every developer.
