How It Works

Three steps to connect.

No proprietary dashboards. Just GitLab, markdown, and an automated pipeline that does the heavy lifting.

01

Submit via GitLab

Open an issue or paste a job URL. Templates mean you never have to edit raw JSON files.

02

Automated Processing

Our CI pipeline builds listing data from markdown and scrapes structured details from supported job-site URLs.

03

Connect & Grow

Your listing goes live on the site, reaching a community of security-minded engineers and employers.
