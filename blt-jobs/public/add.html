Contribute Git-managed open data

Open a GitLab issue or propose Markdown. Maintainers review public fields before listings and profiles are published.

Easiest

Post a job from URL

Open an issue and paste a public job link. The link becomes input for a reviewable Markdown draft; maintainers publish only after checking the generated fields.

Do not include private applicant notes, private phone numbers, identity documents, or secrets. Published job data is public Git data.

Quick tutorial

Post a job (fill the form)

Open an issue and fill in company, title, location, description, and how to apply. Maintainers review the issue, then the accepted record is committed as Markdown.

Open issue: Post job (form)

Create a job seeker profile

Open an issue and fill in your name, headline, skills, and links. Your profile is public Markdown once accepted and appears on the Find Talent page.

Only include email, resume links, and contact routes you intentionally want published in this repository and static site.
