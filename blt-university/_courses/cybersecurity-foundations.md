This course is for anyone who uses a phone, computer, or online account. Each lesson uses a realistic situation and a short knowledge check so you can practice a safer response immediately.

You do not need to write code. The course follows three beginner-friendly steps: recognize what looks unusual, decide what to do safely, and protect yourself by taking the right action. The goal is to build habits you can use in everyday situations.
