Marcus Rodriguez

Penetration Testing & Red Team Operations

Biography

Marcus Rodriguez is a veteran penetration tester and red team leader with 12 years of experience in offensive security. He specializes in advanced penetration testing, social engineering, and adversary simulation.

Professional Background

Marcus has conducted hundreds of penetration tests for Fortune 500 companies, government agencies, and critical infrastructure organizations. He currently leads a red team at a major cybersecurity firm and is a certified OSCP, OSCE, and GXPN holder.

Expertise Areas

  • Red Team Operations

  • Physical Security Assessments

Teaching Philosophy

Achievements

Teaching Style

Marcus is known for his challenging labs, real-world scenarios, and commitment to student success. His courses have a high pass rate for professional certifications because they go beyond theory to develop practical skills.
